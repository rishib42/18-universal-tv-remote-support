Support

Universal Remote controls Roku, Samsung, LG, Sony and other Google TV / Android TV sets from your iPhone over Wi-Fi.

Need help or found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your TV brand and model, and your iOS version.

Common questions

The app can't find my TV

Make sure your iPhone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, and turn the TV on. Check that Local Network access is allowed in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Local Network. You can also add the TV by its IP address.

My TV asks for a code or permission

Samsung, LG and Google TV sets ask you to accept the remote the first time. Accept on the TV, or enter the code it shows. This happens only once.

How do I turn the TV on?

Add the TV's MAC address in Settings › your TV to wake it with Wake-on-LAN. Some TVs also need "Turn on via Wi-Fi" or a similar option enabled in their own settings.

What is Spotlight?

Point your camera at the TV and tap Scan to see the title, rating and cast of what's on. The free trial includes 5 scans a day and the subscription includes 10.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open iOS Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Universal Remote › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current month ends to avoid being charged.

I reinstalled the app or changed phones

Open the app and tap Restore purchases on the subscription screen. Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
