Privacy Policy

Universal Remote · Last updated 2 November 2026

In short

Universal Remote has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your TVs and settings stay on your iPhone. The only data that leaves your iPhone is the Spotlight snapshot, which is used once to identify the show and is not kept.

Controlling your TV

The app finds TVs on your Wi-Fi and sends remote commands to them directly, over your local network. These commands do not go through our servers. Saved TVs, pairing details and settings are stored on your iPhone only.

Spotlight

When you tap Scan, the app takes one photo of your TV screen and sends it over an encrypted connection to our Spotlight server. The server sends the image to an AI model (OpenAI's GPT model, through OpenRouter) to read the title on screen, then looks up details on TMDB and OMDb.

  • The photo is processed in memory and is not saved by our server.
  • It is not linked to you, your Apple ID or your device.
  • Our AI providers process it only to answer that request, under their own data policies.
  • Recent results are saved on your iPhone only, and are removed when you delete the app.

The camera is used only while Spotlight is open, and only after you allow camera access.

Subscriptions

Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not see your name, email or payment details. A daily count of Spotlight scans is kept on your iPhone to apply the daily limit.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
